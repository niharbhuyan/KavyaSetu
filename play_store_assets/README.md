## 🚀 How to Upload to Google Play Console

1. Navigate to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Select your app **Kavya Setu** (or create a new app listing).
3. Go to **Grow** -> **Store presence** -> **Main store listing**.
4. In **App details**:
   - Paste the App Title, Short Description, and Full Description from `metadata_listing.json`.
5. In **Graphics**:
   - **App icon**: Upload `play_store_512_icon.png`.
   - **Feature graphic**: Upload `play_store_feature_graphic_1024x500.png`.
   - **Phone screenshots**: Upload all 4 screenshots in order (`screenshot_1` through `screenshot_4`).
6. Click **Save** and submit for review.

Executive Summary: Kavya Setu ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. Kavya Setu is built with an offline-first architecture. Your personal saved poems, custom anthologies, reading streaks, and notes reside locally on your device in a secure SQLite/Room database. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information You Store Locally

  • Saved Poetry and Favorites: Couplets, Ghazals, Nazms, and Dohas you mark as favorite or save to custom anthologies.
  • Reading Progress & Streaks: Verse counts, reading streaks, and bookmarks to encourage literary discovery.
  • Custom Poetry Creations: Poems composed in the AI Studio or custom cards designed in the Calligraphy Studio.

All of this data is stored in your device's private sandbox storage using Android Room Database. It does not leave your device unless you choose to share an image or verse.

B. Information Processed via Third-Party Services

When you explicitly use connected online features, certain data is transmitted strictly to provide the requested service:

  • Gemini AI Poetry Composition: When you generate poetry or request meter/rhyme analysis (Bahr, Radif, Kafiya), your prompt text is transmitted via Google's Gemini API endpoints strictly to generate the poetic output. Prompts are not used by us to train models or profile users.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): If enabled, a non-personally-identifiable Firebase installation token is used solely to deliver the optional "Shayari of the Day" notification.

2. Device Permissions and Justifications

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies and the principle of least privilege, Kavya Setu only requests permissions essential for core features:

Permission Type Purpose & Policy Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Required for fetching multilingual poetry updates, streaming audio recitations, and Gemini AI poetic composition.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Checks network connectivity to switch between online streaming and offline vault mode gracefully.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Dangerous (Runtime prompt, Android 13+) Allows optional delivery of the daily "Shayari of the Day" and community Mehfil notifications. You can disable this anytime in App Settings.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Dangerous (Runtime prompt) Used exclusively when you choose to record your voice recitation during interactive Sher-baazi or virtual Mehfil recitals. Audio is processed locally or played in your session; it is never secretly recorded or uploaded without your explicit action.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal (Install-time) Provides subtle haptic feedback during interactive Wah-Wah applause and rhythm interactions.
Photo & Storage Zero-Permission Compliance: In accordance with Google Play's Android Photo Picker and Scoped Storage policies, Kavya Setu does not request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). When you save calligraphy cards, the app uses Android's zero-permission MediaStore API to safely place images into your device's public Pictures/KavyaSetu_PlayStore folder.

3. Third-Party Services and Privacy Standards

Our app utilizes certified third-party SDKs that process data in accordance with their respective privacy standards:

  • Google Gemini API: Generative AI processing for poetry creation. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (FCM): Push notification delivery. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Android Text-to-Speech (TTS): Local speech synthesizer for spoken verse renditions.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your content is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete individual saved verses, anthologies, or generated poems anytime from within the app.
  • You can clear all app data or uninstall the application at any time, which permanently purges all local databases, cache, and preferences from your device.
  • To request inquiry or deletion of any support correspondence, contact the developer at niharbhuyan@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Kavya Setu is a general audience literature and poetry application with a content rating of Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Security Measures

We implement standard industry measures to safeguard your information:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption for all network communication with external APIs.
  • Zero dynamic code loading (DCL) of executable binaries.
  • Sandboxed Android internal storage architecture preventing other applications from accessing your poetry vault.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date at this URL and inside the application's Profile settings.

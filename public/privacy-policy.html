1. Introduction

Welcome to Kavya Setu (काव्यसेतु) ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a rich, cultural, and offline-first poetry, shayari, and literary experience while honoring and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application (Package Name: com.aistudio.shayari.m3).

2. Offline-First & Zero Personal Data Collection

Kavya Setu is built with an offline-first architecture:

  • No Account Required: You can browse, read, bookmark, copy, listen to, and compose poetry completely anonymously without providing an email, phone number, or password.
  • Local Storage: Your personal bookmarks, custom written shayaris, favorites, audio preferences, and downloaded collections are saved strictly on your device using local SQLite (Room Database). We do not store or transmit your private verses to our servers.

4. Permissions Requested by the App

Kavya Setu only asks for minimal permissions needed for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load community poems, update the curated catalogue, and render AdMob banner ads.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide subtle haptic feedback during interactive features like the Virtual Mehfil applause.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission on Android 13+ to receive the daily "Morning Couplet" reminder (can be disabled at any time in App Settings).
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, GPS location, or external storage files.

5. AI Studio & Gemini Assistant

When you choose to use the optional "Sukhanwar AI Studio" to generate rhymes or analyze meter (Bahr), your requested prompt is processed via Google's Gemini API to generate the literary response. No sensitive personal data or private device telemetry is bundled with these prompt requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Kavya Setu does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All literary works are cultural, artistic, and suitable for general audiences.

7. Google Play Policy & Data Safety Compliance

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety declaration:

  • Data Collected: Approximate location and Device IDs (collected only by Google AdMob for ad delivery and fraud prevention).
  • Data Shared: None shared with third parties other than Google AdMob advertising networks.
  • Security: All network communications use standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

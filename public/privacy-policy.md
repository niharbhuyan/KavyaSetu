## 1. Introduction
Kavya Setu ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing an offline-first poetry, shayari, and literary culture experience while respecting and safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information handled when you use the Kavya Setu Android application.

## 2. Offline-First & Personal Data Collection
- **No Personal Data Stored Remotely:** We do not require registration or personal identification (such as your name, email, phone number, or address) to read, bookmark, or create poems.
- **Local SQLite Database:** All your custom verses, bookmarks, audio settings, and downloads are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database (Room).

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

- **Google Play Services:**
  - Used for essential security and app distribution compliance: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Permissions Used
- `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Needed to load new shayari collections and display AdMob banner ads.
- `VIBRATE`: Provides haptic feedback during interactive features (e.g. applause in Virtual Mehfil).
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Optional permission on Android 13+ for the daily morning couplet reminder.
- **Zero Sensitive Permissions:** The app does NOT request camera, contacts, SMS, precise GPS location, or external files access.

## 5. AI Features (Sukhanwar AI Studio)
When you submit prompts for rhyme or meter analysis, text is sent to Google's Gemini API over secure HTTPS. No personal identifying information is transmitted.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our app contains classical and contemporary literature and does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 years of age.

## 7. Data Safety Declaration (Google Play)
- **Data Encrypted in Transit:** All network requests use TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption.
- **Data Retention & Deletion:** Since all user content is stored locally on the device, you can delete all data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
